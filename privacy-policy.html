Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

Puzzlebox (Android application ID: com.etriq.puzzlebox, Current version: 1.2.0) is a collection of 15 free offline puzzle games, designed and developed by q04ti. The 15 included games are: Chess, Sudoku, Daily Five, Connections, The Mini, Spelling Bee, Crossword, Strands, Pips, Tiles, Letter Boxed, Vertex, Nonogram, Binary, and Cages.

2. Data Collection and Tracking

Puzzlebox is an offline puzzle game application. It operates entirely locally on your device. Specifically:

  • No user account is required.
  • No advertising SDK is used.
  • No analytics SDK is used.
  • No tracking or telemetry is used.
  • The app does not have a backend that collects user data.

3. Local Storage and Progress

Game progress, settings, statistics, streaks, and related gameplay data are stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app removes its locally stored app data according to Android's normal app-data behavior. If your Android device is configured to automatically back up app data, this data may be backed up by your device provider.

4. PDF Exports and Sharing

PDF statistics exports are generated locally on the device. When you choose to export or share a PDF, Android's native sharing mechanism is used to let you select another application or destination. Puzzlebox itself does not upload the PDF to a server.

5. Permissions

No contacts, location, microphone, camera, SMS, phone, or other sensitive permissions are used by the app. The app only requests the standard Android vibration permission to provide haptic feedback during gameplay.

6. Contact

For questions or further information, please visit q04ti.dev. Do not include private or sensitive information in public messages.
