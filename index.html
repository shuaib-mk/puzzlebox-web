Frequently Asked Questions

Is the app free?
Yes. Puzzlebox is completely free to download and play. There are no hidden fees, subscriptions, or microtransactions.
Is an account required?
No. You can open the app and start playing instantly. We don't ask for your email, and there are no login screens.
Does it work offline?
Absolutely. Puzzlebox is a 100% offline experience. You can play anywhere, whether you're on an airplane or deep underground on the subway.
Is it available on Android and iOS?
Puzzlebox is currently available exclusively for Android devices via the Google Play Store.
Does it contain ads?
No. We believe puzzle games require deep focus. There are no banner ads, video ads, or interrupting pop-ups anywhere in the app.
What permissions does it require?
Puzzlebox respects your privacy and requires zero sensitive permissions (no camera, location, or contacts). The only standard permission it uses is vibration for tactile haptic feedback.
How often is it updated?
We are in active development and update the app regularly with new puzzle games, performance improvements, and community suggestions.
